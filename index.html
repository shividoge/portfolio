01

Autonomous Hydroponic Growth Chamber

NASA Kennedy Space Center Award · SSEF

Independent research

A closed-loop plant growth system built to test whether a crop cycle could survive conditions relevant to spaceflight.

I designed a custom I²C PCB around a Teensy microcontroller and a BME680 environmental sensor, then paired the telemetry with an OpenCV vision pipeline. Combining HSV and Excess Green Index segmentation gave a continuous read on plant health, and a regression model correlated the sensor and visual streams against real growth outcomes across repeated trials.
