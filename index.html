About

I’m a computer engineering student at the University of Florida, and I like the place where software meets a signal. Before UF I designed a sensor board, built a closed-loop grow chamber around it, and took the research to a NASA Kennedy Space Center award. I also got my school’s robotics program competing again after years away. Now I’m writing CPU RTL in the Smart Systems Lab and designing the whole board for IEEE’s SoutheastCon robot.
